# i swear on a stack of — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

## The short version

i swear on a stack of does not collect any data. There is nothing to collect, share, sell, or lose.

## How the app works

The app runs entirely on your device. When you type a noun, the picture is drawn locally by Apple's on-device image generation (Apple Intelligence). The words you type, the pictures you create, and everything else the app does never leave your phone.

Specifically:

- **No accounts.** The app has no sign in, registration, or profile.
- **No network.** The app makes no network connections and contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools.
- **No data collected.** No personal data, or any other data, is collected about you or your device. Nothing is transmitted, stored on a server, or shared with anyone.
- **Your pictures stay on your device.** Only you can access them; you can share them from your Photos app, like any other picture.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, no data about children is collected either.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that collects data, I will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before the change ships.
